Imagine joining a project with a thousand unfamiliar details. You ask an agent one useful question, and its answer reveals five more directions worth exploring. You follow two of them. Each creates more questions. A few turns later, the conversation is full of answers, but it is hard to see what is still open, and easy to ask the same question twice.

Rhyza turns those scattered conversations into a visible work map. You can see what is understood, what still needs attention, and where to continue next.

Your material is not limited to code. Add project files, documents, notes, papers, or books. The agent searches the local sources you provide, reads the relevant passages, and keeps answers grounded in the work.

Every conversation turn is a node. When an answer opens a new direction, branch directly from that turn. Each branch carries its own focused context. Right-click a node to mark it In progress, Pending, Completed, or On hold, so unfinished work remains visible instead of disappearing into the transcript.

Focused branches also remove unrelated history from the active context. The agent receives the relevant path, not every previous topic. That means less noise, more focused answers, and fewer tokens spent on work that does not belong to the question.

You can return days later to the exact branch where you stopped, with its context and status intact.

Rhyza also turns useful answers into reusable knowledge. Entities, relationships, diagrams, and source references stay connected. When an existing concept appears again, you can open it in place, inspect its details, and reuse it as context for the next decision.

Rhyza makes the shape of knowledge work visible: branch without losing the original question, track what remains, preserve context, and keep useful knowledge moving forward.
