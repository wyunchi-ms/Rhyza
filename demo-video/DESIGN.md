# KnowBranch Demo Video Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Use the visual language of the KnowBranch desktop product: quiet, precise, and work-focused. The video should feel like a polished product walkthrough rather than a launch commercial. Real application screenshots are the hero asset. Motion guides attention through source retrieval, Mermaid diagrams, knowledge capture, and session branching with restrained camera moves, thin rules, cursor cues, and concise labels.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#F7F7F5`
- Primary text: `#212121`
- Secondary text: `#64748B`
- Product surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Border: `#D8DEE6`
- Accent / knowledge: `#0E9674` (video-safe contrast adjustment of the product accent)
- Technical dark: `#0F172A`

## Typography

- UI and narration titles: `Segoe UI Variable`, `Microsoft YaHei UI`, sans-serif
- Technical labels and counters: `Cascadia Mono`, `Consolas`, monospace
- Headlines use 750-800 weight; supporting copy uses 350-450 weight.
- Letter spacing is always `0`.

## Motion

- Primary transition: 0.4-second horizontal push with `power3.inOut`.
- Accent transition: 0.55-second focus pull for major topic changes.
- Screenshot movement: slow 2-5% scale and 40-100px pan, with short focus outlines.
- Captions: phrase-level fade and 12px rise; keywords use the accent color.
- Keep the interface readable for at least 3 seconds after each camera move.

## What NOT to Do

- No neon, gradient text, particle fields, bokeh, or decorative blobs.
- No generic AI imagery or fake product screens.
- No oversized marketing cards or nested cards.
- No frantic cursor movement, glitch transitions, or continuous zooming.
- No feature-list scene with every capability carrying equal visual weight.
